# Fictional Cloud Kites

## Shape Memory

In this fictional flight guide, cloud kites maintain their shape with flexible silver ribs sewn into a lightweight frame. The ribs spring back after a gust bends the kite.

## Tether Pattern

A braided violet tether distributes tension across three fictional anchor loops. The loop pattern prevents one edge from carrying the full pull.

## Light Markers

Tiny painted stars act only as fictional alignment markers; they do not power or steer the kite.
